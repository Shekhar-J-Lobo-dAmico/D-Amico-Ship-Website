ABOUT US

D’AMICO SHIP ISHIMA INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED HAS OPERATED IN INDIA FOR NEARLY TWO DECADES, WITH ITS HEAD OFFICE IN MUMBAI AND A FIELD OFFICE IN DELHI SUPPORTING MANNING OPERATIONS FOR THE D’AMICO GROUP AND THIRD-PARTY SHIPOWNERS. THE COMPANY SPECIALISES IN RECRUITING, DEPLOYING, AND TRAINING QUALIFIED AND EXPERIENCED OFFICERS AND CREW WHILE EMPHASISING WELFARE, TRANSPARENCY, AND STRONG SHORE-BASED SUPPORT.


WITH A ROSTER OF APPROXIMATELY 2,000 INDIAN SEAFARERS AND A RETENTION RATE EXCEEDING 80%, THE COMPANY REFLECTS HIGH OPERATIONAL STANDARDS AND PROFESSIONAL SHIP MANAGEMENT. BACKED BY A MOTIVATED WORKFORCE AND EFFICIENT ONSHORE MANAGEMENT, THE ORGANISATION AIMS TO EXPAND ALONGSIDE THE CONTINUED GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT OF INDIAN SEAFARERS IN THE GLOBAL MARITIME INDUSTRY.
